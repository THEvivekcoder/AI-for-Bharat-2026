Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions

How do I register on BharatSahayak?

To register on BharatSahayak:

  1. Click on "Login / Register" button
  2. Enter your mobile number
  3. Select your preferred language
  4. Click "Register"
  5. You will receive an OTP on your mobile
  6. Enter the OTP to complete registration

Can I register without a mobile number?

Currently, a mobile number is required for registration as we use OTP-based authentication for security. This ensures that only you can access your account and personal information.

How do I check my eligibility for schemes?

To check eligibility:

  1. Complete your profile with accurate information
  2. Go to "Eligible Schemes" section
  3. Our AI will automatically match you with schemes
  4. You can also search for specific schemes and check eligibility

What information do I need to provide?

You need to provide:

  • Age and Gender
  • Education Level
  • Occupation
  • Income Bracket
  • Location (State, District, Pincode)

This information helps us match you with the most relevant schemes.

How do I apply for a scheme?

To apply for a scheme:

  1. Find the scheme you're eligible for
  2. Click "View Details" to see requirements
  3. Click "Apply Now"
  4. You'll be redirected to the official application portal
  5. Follow the instructions on the official portal

How long does application processing take?

Processing time varies by scheme and department. Typically:

  • Central schemes: 15-30 days
  • State schemes: 7-21 days
  • Emergency schemes: 3-7 days

You can track your application status in the "My Applications" section.

How do I use the voice assistant?

To use the voice assistant:

  1. Go to "Voice Assistant" section
  2. Select your preferred language
  3. Click the microphone button
  4. Speak your query clearly
  5. Wait for the response

You can ask about schemes, eligibility, application process, and more!

Which languages are supported?

BharatSahayak supports multiple Indian languages:

  • Hindi (हिंदी)
  • English
  • Bengali (বাংলা)
  • Tamil (தமிழ்)
  • Telugu (తెలుగు)
  • Marathi (मराठी)
  • And more coming soon!

Is my data secure?

Yes! We take data security very seriously:

  • All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • We use AWS Cognito for secure authentication
  • No data is shared with third parties
  • Regular security audits are conducted
  • Compliant with Government of India data protection guidelines

Can I use BharatSahayak on mobile?

Yes! BharatSahayak is fully responsive and works on:

  • Mobile phones (Android & iOS)
  • Tablets
  • Desktop computers
  • Laptops

You can also install it as a Progressive Web App (PWA) for offline access!

Can I use it offline?

Limited offline functionality is available:

  • View previously loaded schemes
  • Access saved information
  • View your profile

Internet connection is required for:

  • Searching new schemes
  • Checking eligibility
  • Applying for schemes
  • Using voice assistant
