Our Services

BharatSahayak provides comprehensive digital services designed to empower rural citizens with easy access to government schemes, agricultural guidance, and essential information in their preferred language.

Voice Assistant

Interact with our AI-powered voice assistant in your native language. Simply speak your query and receive instant responses. No typing required, making it accessible for users with limited literacy or those who prefer voice interaction.

Scheme Discovery

Search and discover from 400+ central and state government schemes. Our intelligent search system helps you find relevant schemes based on your profile, location, and specific needs, ensuring you never miss out on benefits you're entitled to.

Eligibility Checker

Instantly verify your eligibility for various government schemes. Our AI-powered system analyzes your profile against scheme criteria and provides detailed eligibility reports, saving you time and helping you apply with confidence.

Agriculture Advisory

Access real-time agricultural guidance including crop recommendations, market prices from nearby mandis, weather forecasts, and farming best practices. Empowering farmers with data-driven insights for better decision-making.

Multilingual Support

Available in 10+ Indian languages including Hindi, English, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, and Punjabi. Breaking language barriers to ensure every citizen can access government services comfortably.

Offline Access

Access cached information even without internet connectivity. Our progressive web app technology ensures that essential information remains available in areas with poor or intermittent network coverage.

How It Works

Getting started with BharatSahayak is simple and straightforward. Follow these three easy steps to begin accessing government services and schemes.

1

Register Your Account

Create your account using your mobile number. Verify through OTP for secure access. The registration process is quick, taking less than 2 minutes to complete.

2

Complete Your Profile

Provide basic information about yourself through our simple 4-step wizard. This helps us personalize scheme recommendations and ensure you receive relevant information.

3

Access Services

Start exploring schemes, checking eligibility, getting agricultural advice, and accessing all services in your preferred language through voice or text interface.
