Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 6, 2026

1. Introduction

BharatSahayak is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you use our platform. By using BharatSahayak, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We collect the following personal information:

  • Contact Information: Mobile number for authentication
  • Profile Information: Age, gender, education level, occupation, income bracket
  • Location Data: State, district, and pincode
  • Language Preference: Your preferred language for communication

2.2 Usage Information

We automatically collect:

  • Device information (type, operating system, browser)
  • IP address and location data
  • Pages visited and features used
  • Search queries and interactions
  • Voice recordings (when using voice assistant)
  • Timestamps of activities

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Authenticate your identity and secure your account
  • Match you with eligible government schemes
  • Provide personalized recommendations
  • Process voice queries and provide responses
  • Improve our services and user experience
  • Generate analytics and usage statistics
  • Communicate important updates and notifications
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Prevent fraud and ensure platform security

4. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Authentication: Secure OTP-based authentication via AWS Cognito
  • Access Control: Strict role-based access controls for our team
  • Regular Audits: Periodic security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Secure Infrastructure: Hosted on AWS with enterprise-grade security
  • Data Backup: Regular automated backups with encryption

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell your personal information. We may share your data only in the following circumstances:

  • Government Departments: With relevant departments for scheme application processing (with your consent)
  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party service providers who assist in platform operations (under strict confidentiality agreements)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government authority
  • Aggregated Data: Anonymous, aggregated statistics for research and policy-making

6. Voice Data

When you use our voice assistant, we record and process your voice input to provide responses. Voice recordings are:

  • Processed using secure speech recognition services
  • Stored temporarily for quality improvement
  • Deleted after 90 days unless required for legal purposes
  • Never shared with third parties for marketing

7. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Maintain your login session
  • Analyze platform usage and performance
  • Improve user experience

You can control cookies through your browser settings, but disabling them may affect platform functionality.

8. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured format
  • Objection: Object to certain data processing activities
  • Withdrawal: Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@bharatsahayak.gov.in

9. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as:

  • Your account is active
  • Needed to provide services to you
  • Required by law or for legal purposes
  • Necessary for legitimate business purposes

After account deletion, we may retain anonymized data for statistical purposes.

10. Children's Privacy

BharatSahayak is not intended for children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are under 18, please use this platform only with parental or guardian consent.

11. Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to external websites (e.g., official scheme application portals). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites. Please review their privacy policies before providing any information.

12. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@bharatsahayak.gov.in
  • Phone: 1800-XXX-XXXX (Toll Free)
  • Address: Data Protection Officer, Ministry of Electronics & IT, New Delhi, India

Your privacy and data security are our top priorities.
