The Problem

Over 1,500 government schemes exist in India, but 70% of eligible citizens remain unaware. Complex eligibility criteria, language barriers, and scattered information prevent millions from accessing benefits they deserve.

Our Solution

BharatSahayak uses AI to simplify scheme discovery. Voice-first interface in 10+ Indian languages, intelligent eligibility matching, and personalized recommendations ensure every citizen can find and access the right schemes effortlessly.
